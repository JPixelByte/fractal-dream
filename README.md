A self-contained, browser-native ambient soundscape and motion visualizer designed to lull the brain into deep relaxation, mitigate eye strain, and offer a soothing digital workspace after intensive technical grinds.

## 🔬 The Computer Science & Mathematical Core

### 1. Number Theory (Counting Prime Sheep)
The engine generates its spatial positioning by hunting down Prime Numbers using an optimized trial division routine. The distribution properties of primes prevent the stars from traveling in rigid patterns, structuring them into loose, organic star clusters instead.

### 2. Neuro-Acoustic Entrainment (Binaural Beats)
By initializing dual standalone oscillators via the web architecture, the engine routes a **100Hz pure sine wave** to the left ear canal and a matching **102Hz or 106Hz frequency** to the right ear. Wearing headphones forces the brain to reconcile the variance, subconsciously manufacturing an internal **2Hz Delta wave** or **6Hz Theta wave** to simulate deep, restorative sleep cycles.

### 3. DSP Precipitation Synthesis (Procedural Rain)
To create realistic weather elements without an `.mp3` footprint, a custom random transient injector buffer (`addRainPatter`) scans a live 44.1kHz audio data array. It injects mathematically decaying exponential curves ($Math.exp$) directly onto the white/pink power spectral distributions, translating raw floating-point calculations into distinct, comforting sound impulses that mimic rain striking glass.
